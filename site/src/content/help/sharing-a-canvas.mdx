## Inviting by email

Open the Share dialog on any canvas you own and enter a collaborator's email address. If they already have a verified openmaths account under that email, they get access immediately; otherwise the invite shows as pending until they sign up and verify with that address.

## Inviting by link instead

Generate a share link from the same dialog if you'd rather not collect email addresses up front — anyone with the link can join with the role the link grants. You can revoke a link at any time, which immediately invalidates it for anyone who hasn't already joined; people who already joined through it keep their access unless you remove them separately.

## Editor vs. viewer

- **Editor** can add questions, edit notes, and modify the canvas.
- **Viewer** can see everything — including live updates as an editor works — but can't change anything.

You can change a collaborator's role, or remove them entirely, from the same Share dialog at any time.

## What collaborators see

A canvas you're invited to (as either role) shows up on your own dashboard alongside canvases you own — there's no separate "shared with me" area to check.
