Every step of a diagram walkthrough can now be narrated aloud, synced to the diagram as it plays — turn it on from the playback controls. An optional "active recall pause" briefly stops playback between steps with a short reflection prompt, off by default.

Once a walkthrough has played through, you can export it as a video — with narration included if it was on, or silent otherwise — to share outside the app.

See [how narration and voice playback work](/help/narration-and-playback) for the details.
