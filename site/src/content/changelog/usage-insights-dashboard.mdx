Two new pages under Settings: **Usage** shows your all-time generation history broken down by model, with real per-model cost figures. **Insights** aggregates your own learning patterns — which answer forms you use most, your self-check correction rate, walkthrough-completion counts, and how many exports were narrated vs. silent.

Both work from data already being recorded — this just makes it visible.
