Canvases can now be shared with collaborators, either by email invite or a revocable share link, with editor or viewer roles you can change at any time. Real-time notifications let you know when someone joins, edits, or comments on a canvas you're part of.

See [sharing a canvas with collaborators](/help/sharing-a-canvas) for a full walkthrough.
