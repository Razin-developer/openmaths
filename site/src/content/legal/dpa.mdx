A Data Processing Addendum (DPA) is a contract that governs how we process personal data on behalf of an organization using openmaths — typically needed by schools and businesses subject to GDPR, FERPA, or similar regulations.

## What it covers

A DPA typically specifies: what data is processed and why, sub-processors involved (e.g. the AI model providers used to generate answers), data retention and deletion commitments, and security measures in place.

## Requesting a DPA

We don't publish a one-size-fits-all DPA as static website text — the terms depend on your organization's specific regulatory requirements. If your school or business needs a signed DPA to use openmaths, [contact us](/contact) and we'll work through it with you directly.
