You may not use openmaths to:

- Generate or share content that is illegal, harassing, hateful, or intended to harm someone.

- Upload malicious files or attempt to use the service to distribute malware.

## Enforcement

Violating this policy may result in a warning, temporary suspension, or permanent termination of your account, depending on severity. We aim to be reasonable and give the benefit of the doubt for first-time, low-severity issues.
