**Last updated: September 5, 2026**

## What we collect

- **Account information**: email address, display name, and a hashed password (never the password itself).
- **Content you create**: questions you ask, canvases, notes, and any files you attach.
- **Usage data**: which features you use and how often, so we can improve the product and enforce fair-use limits.

## What we don't do

We don't sell your data to third parties. We don't use your canvas content to train models beyond what's needed to generate your own answers.

## How we use your data

- To provide the service itself — generating answers, storing your canvases, syncing across sessions.
- To communicate with you about your account (verification emails, password resets, security notices).
- To improve the product, using aggregated or anonymized usage patterns where possible.

## Sharing with third parties

Generating an answer involves sending your question to an AI model provider. We don't share your account information (email, password) with that provider — only the content needed to answer your question.

## Your rights

You can request a copy of your data or its deletion at any time by contacting us. Deleting your account removes your canvases and account information from active systems.

See our separate [Cookie Policy](/cookies) for details on what cookies we use and why.

## Changes to this policy

We'll update the "last updated" date above when this policy changes, and notify active users of material changes.
