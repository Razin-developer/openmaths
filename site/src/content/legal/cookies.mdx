## What cookies we use

**Essential (always on).** A session cookie that keeps you logged in to the product app. Without it, you'd need to sign in again on every page load. This cookie can't be disabled without breaking login.

**Preferences.** Your theme choice (light/dark) is stored in your browser's local storage, not a cookie — it never leaves your device.

**Analytics (opt-in where required by law).** If enabled, privacy-friendly, cookie-free analytics (aggregate page-view counts, not individual tracking) help us understand which pages and features are actually used.

## What we don't use

No third-party advertising cookies, no cross-site tracking pixels.

## Managing cookies

Since the only cookie this site sets is the essential login session cookie, there's nothing to opt out of without also logging you out. Your browser's own cookie settings can block or clear it at any time — you'll just need to sign in again afterward.
